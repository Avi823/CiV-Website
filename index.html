Welcome to CiV - A Cybersecurity and Computer Science Community!

About Us

CiV is a for high-schoolers, by high-schoolers organization that aims to introduce students to the world of cybersecurity and computer science. We provide resources, workshops, and events to help students develop their skills and explore career opportunities in these fields.

Events and Highlights

During November, we hosted a CiV x MIT Lincoln Laboratory talk about applying to the lab's summer programs, and featured an MIT LL researcher!

Some upcoming guest talks that we are planning include a university CS professor and a talk on careers by the NJ Office of Homeland Security.

What we do:

  • Organize workshops introducing cybersecurity to STEM under-resourced students.
  • Host bimonthly hackathons and coding competitions with prizes.
  • Present guest lectures from industry professionals and academics.
  • Share learning resources for all students as a beginner-friendly and welcoming community.
  • Currently organizing a 2027 in-person hackathon + CTF planned for Fall 2027 (more details to come!)
